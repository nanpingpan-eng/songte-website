How We Help Confirm the Right Cylinder

Three steps to reduce the risk of ordering the wrong part.

1

Part Number Verification

Send the cylinder part number or machine model (e.g. HITACHI ZX330-3G). We confirm the matching assembly and any available alternatives.

2

Dimension Confirmation

For custom or unmatched cylinders, share rod diameter, stroke, mounting style or old-cylinder photos. We confirm drawings before production.

3

Pre-shipment Confirmation

Photos, video and inspection reports are arranged per order so you know what you receive before it ships.

Before Ordering

  • Send the part number and machine model; if unsure, send a photo of the nameplate or old cylinder and key dimensions.
  • Confirm condition, lead time, payment terms and warranty in writing before we proceed.
  • For custom products, we confirm drawings and dimensions in writing before production starts.
