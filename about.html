About SongTe

Guangzhou SongTe Engineering Machinery CO.,LTD — excavator hydraulic cylinder and parts supplier.

Guangzhou SongTe Engineering Machinery CO.,LTD develops and sells hydraulic cylinders, hydraulic pumps, swing / travel motors, control valves, engine assemblies and other crawler excavator spare parts. We specialize in the production of hydraulic cylinder assemblies and support customization, and we work by part number so customers can identify the exact replacement they need.

We support customers with dimension confirmation for custom cylinders, part-number identification, and pre-shipment photos, video and inspection arranged per order. Our products have been exported to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, East Asia and Africa.
