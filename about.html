About SongTe

A high-tech enterprise for excavator hydraulic parts based in Guangzhou, China.

Guangzhou SongTe Engineering Machinery CO.,LTD is a high-tech enterprise integrating scientific research, supporting and technical consulting services. Our company mainly develops and sells hydraulic cylinder, hydraulic pump, swing / travel motor, control valve, engine assembly and other crawler excavator spare parts and has rich sales experience in the excavator hydraulic industry. We have a professional R&D and sales team, and our products are highly praised by customers at home and abroad. If you have requirements for customized products, welcome to cooperate with us.
