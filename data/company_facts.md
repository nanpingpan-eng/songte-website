## 6. Main product line (one-line summary)

- SongTe designs and supplies **hydraulic cylinder assemblies, hydraulic main pumps, swing and travel (final drive) motors, control valves, complete diesel engine assemblies, oil seals / seal kits, hydraulic breakers, solenoid valves, radiators and other crawler excavator spare parts**, with customization support and export to Southeast Asia, the Middle East, East Asia and Africa.
  - Sources: Catalog45 p.2–p.3; 油缸油封属具等目录.pdf p.2, p.10, p.13.

- Swing motors: M2X150, M2X63, M5X130 (油缸油封属具等目录.pdf p.14).
- Engine assemblies: YANMAR (4TNV98 etc.), KUBOTA (Z482, V3800T etc.), ISUZU (4BG1T, 4HK1, 6HK1), MITSUBISHI (S4ST/S4KT/S6ST), CUMMINS/6D107/QSB6.7, Caterpillar (3066, C7, C9, C13, C18 etc.) (Catalog45 p.30–p.45; Engine.pdf).
